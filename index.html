Features

Explore the universe from the surface up

Every feature is grounded in real published data from NASA, ESA, and peer-reviewed catalogs.

Spacetime Curvature Grid

Watch a three-dimensional lattice of geodesics bend and warp around each star. The more massive the star, the deeper the well. Sirius, Betelgeuse, and every system in between — visualized in real time as you orbit.

T Coronae Borealis — The Blaze Star

A red giant so close to its white-dwarf companion that hydrogen streams between them in a fiery accretion disk. Watch the mass transfer in 3D, then trigger the nova to see what happens when the pressure ignites.

The Sky From Other Worlds

Stand on Barnard c and find our Sun in the sky — magnitude 1.1, tucked between altered constellations. Visit Wolf 1061 b, a tidally locked world where the sun never rises on the night side. Every sky is computed from real stellar positions.

Eclipse Chasers

Every total and hybrid solar eclipse through 2051, city by city. Each path checked against NASA data with local timings, band widths, and a "Remind me" notification one month and one day before totality.

Scope Matchmaker

Three questions, one honest recommendation. No brand deals — just the setups astronomy clubs point beginners toward. Then see what each world actually looks like through a backyard telescope.

Red Night Mode

Preserves your night vision at the telescope. The entire interface shifts to deep red — no blue light leaking into your dark-adapted eyes. Battery saver, softer haptics, and unit preferences all in one place.
